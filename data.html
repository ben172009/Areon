Data Usage Guidelines

When using our datasets, please adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Cite the original research publications
  • Maintain participant confidentiality by not attempting to re-identify anonymized individuals
  • Report any errors or issues found in the datasets
  • Do not redistribute the data without permission
  • Share your findings and derivative works with our research group
