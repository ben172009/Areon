About AREON

AREON presents a local study of BeteSeb Academy Ayertena Branch students, focusing on the effect of peer interaction, teacher support, and school social interaction on academic performance. Chapter 1: Introduction

The study examines how peer interaction, teacher-student relationships, and school social interaction affect academic performance of Grade 9 and 10 students at BeteSeb Academy. It highlights the importance of social environments, positive peer groups, and supportive teacher behavior for student motivation and achievement.

Chapter 2: Literature Review

This chapter reviews Social Constructivism, Self-Determination Theory, Attachment Theory, and Ecological Systems Theory. It shows that academic learning is shaped by collaborative peer dialogue, teacher support, and a positive school climate.

Chapter 3: Methodology

A quantitative, correlational, cross-sectional design was used with 236 Grade 9 and 10 students. The research applied stratified sampling, questionnaires, descriptive statistics, and Pearson correlation to measure relationships among peer interaction, teacher support, and academic performance.

Chapter 4: Results & Discussion

Findings reveal that middle and high achievers report stronger peer collaboration and teacher support. Peer interaction, teacher interaction, and school climate all show significant positive correlations, while conflict and distraction remain important challenges to address.

Chapter 5: Conclusions & Recommendations

The study concludes that a supportive school social environment, positive teacher relations, and structured peer collaboration can improve academic outcomes. Recommendations include peer-support programs, teacher training on communication, and school-level activities that strengthen belonging.

Key AREON Insights

Positive peer groups matter.

Students who discuss lessons, study together, and encourage each other tend to be more engaged and perform better academically.

Teacher support is a differentiator.

Respectful, approachable teachers who provide feedback help students feel confident and motivated to perform well.

The school environment is the context.

A healthy social climate amplifies the benefits of peer and teacher interactions while minimizing conflict and distraction.

Low achievers need targeted support.

The research shows lower-performing students often report weaker peer collaboration and teacher support, highlighting where interventions are most needed.
