Results & Discussion

This page summarizes the main findings from the AREON study at BeteSeb Academy, Ayertena Branch. The results are based on responses from 236 Grade 9 and 10 students and 12 teachers.

The charts below are interactive Chart.js circles. Hover over any colored segment to see the exact percent values and better understand how the survey responses are distributed.

Key Findings

Peer interaction matters

Students who discuss lessons with classmates and participate in group study tend to show higher achievement than those who study alone.

Teacher support matters

Teacher encouragement, respect, and clear feedback are strongly linked to student confidence and academic success.

School climate matters

Comfort in social interaction and engagement with school activities supports better motivation and learning.

Correlation Findings

Peer & Teacher

Correlation = .421 (p < .01)

Peer & School Climate

Correlation = .460 (p < .01)

Teacher & School Climate

Correlation = .388 (p < .01)

These values show that stronger peer relationships and better teacher interaction are both associated with a more positive school climate and improved academic outcomes.

Conclusions

  • Peer discussion and group study are linked with higher student achievement.
  • Teacher encouragement and support play a key role in student motivation.
  • A welcoming school social environment helps students feel more engaged and confident.
